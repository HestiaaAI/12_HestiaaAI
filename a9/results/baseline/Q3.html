Borrowing and returning tools

inventory · fixed-inventory-01

The shared tool inventory identifies equipment that belongs to the household and its normal storage location. Small hand tools are arranged in labeled bins so a resident can tell whether a missing item is being used or was put away elsewhere. The inventory card includes the tool name, its home bin, and any accessories that travel with it. Before starting a project, check the card and confirm that the necessary pieces are present. Residents leave personal equipment in their own storage unless they have agreed to share it. This keeps household availability records meaningful for everyone. Before borrowing the shared cordless drill, add your name, the tool name, and your expected return time to the Tool Checkout card. The drill normally lives in the blue tool bin in the hallway cupboard.

Borrowing and returning tools

inventory · fixed-inventory-02

Keep the drill bits with the drill while it is checked out. On return, put both the drill and the bits back in that bin and close your checkout entry. If a piece is missing, add a note to the entry before closing it so the next user can check availability. The checkout card records who has the tool while it is away from its normal location. The household checks shared tools during the monthly cupboard review. The reviewer compares open checkout entries with the tools that are physically present and asks borrowers about entries that have passed their expected return time. Items with damaged cases or missing accessories receive an inventory note so residents can avoid relying on an incomplete kit. The review can also identify tools that are rarely used and would benefit from a clearer label.
